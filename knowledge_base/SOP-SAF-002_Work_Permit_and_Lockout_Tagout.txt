SOP-SAF-002  WORK PERMIT AND LOCKOUT-TAGOUT
Coastal Refinery Unit - Fire and Safety
Revision 4 (sample document for demonstration, not a real standard)

1. PURPOSE
No maintenance work may start on process equipment without a valid work permit and positive isolation of all energy sources.

2. WORK PERMIT
2.1 The permit is issued by the shift in-charge of the operating area and is valid for one shift only.
2.2 Hot work needs a separate hot work permit and a gas test showing less than 5 percent of the lower explosive limit.
2.3 Entry into a vessel or exchanger shell needs a confined space entry permit and an oxygen reading between 19.5 and 23.5 percent.

3. LOCKOUT-TAGOUT (LOTO)
3.1 Isolate electrical supply at the motor control centre and apply a personal lock and tag.
3.2 Close and lock process isolation valves. Fit blinds where the fluid is hydrocarbon or toxic.
3.3 Drain, depressurise and purge the equipment. Confirm zero energy before work begins.
3.4 Each person working on the equipment applies their own lock. Locks are removed only by the person who applied them.

4. PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT
4.1 Minimum PPE in process areas: helmet, safety shoes, safety glasses, flame retardant coverall and gloves.
4.2 Use a breathing apparatus where hydrogen sulphide may be present.

5. CLOSING THE PERMIT
5.1 On completion, remove tools and blinds, restore guards, and return the permit to the issuer.
5.2 The issuer inspects the site before the equipment is handed back to operations.
