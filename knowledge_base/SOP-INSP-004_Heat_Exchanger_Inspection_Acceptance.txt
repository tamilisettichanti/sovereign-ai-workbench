SOP-INSP-004  HEAT EXCHANGER INSPECTION - ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
Coastal Refinery Unit - Inspection Department
Revision 3 (sample document for demonstration, not a real standard)

1. PURPOSE
This procedure sets the acceptance limits used when a shell and tube heat exchanger is inspected during a shutdown or turnaround, and states what action is required when a limit is not met.

2. SHELL AND CHANNEL THICKNESS
2.1 Measure wall thickness by ultrasonic gauging at all marked condition monitoring locations.
2.2 The measured minimum thickness must be greater than the minimum required thickness stated on the equipment data sheet.
2.3 Remaining corrosion allowance = measured minimum thickness minus minimum required thickness.
2.4 If the remaining corrosion allowance is 1.0 mm or more, the equipment is acceptable for the normal inspection interval of 48 months.
2.5 If the remaining corrosion allowance is less than 1.0 mm but greater than zero, the equipment is acceptable for continued service only with a reduced inspection interval of 12 months, and a replacement or repair plan must be raised.
2.6 If the measured thickness is at or below the minimum required thickness, the equipment must not be returned to service.
2.7 Corrosion rate (mm per year) = (nominal thickness minus measured minimum thickness) divided by years in service.
2.8 Remaining life (years) = remaining corrosion allowance divided by corrosion rate.

3. TUBE BUNDLE
3.1 Plugged tubes must not exceed 10 percent of the total tube count.
3.2 Between 5 percent and 10 percent plugged, retubing must be planned for the next turnaround.
3.3 Above 10 percent plugged, the bundle must be retubed or replaced before the exchanger is returned to service.

4. GASKETS AND FLANGED JOINTS
4.1 Any gasket found leaking, damaged or hardened must be replaced before recommissioning. Gaskets are never reused.
4.2 Flange faces must be free of radial scoring.

5. CORROSION UNDER INSULATION (CUI)
5.1 Light CUI: surface rust only. Clean and recoat at the next opportunity.
5.2 Moderate CUI: pitting up to 1.0 mm deep. Clean, recoat and re-insulate before recommissioning.
5.3 Severe CUI: pitting deeper than 1.0 mm. Engineering assessment is required before recommissioning.

6. HYDROSTATIC TEST
6.1 Test pressure is 1.3 times the design pressure, held for a minimum of 30 minutes.
6.2 The test is passed when there is no pressure drop and no visible leak.

7. REPORTING
7.1 Findings that need repair expenditure or a reduced inspection interval must be put up for approval using the approval note format in SOP-ADM-011.
