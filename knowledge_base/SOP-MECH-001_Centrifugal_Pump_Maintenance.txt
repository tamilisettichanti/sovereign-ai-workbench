SOP-MECH-001  CENTRIFUGAL PUMP MAINTENANCE PROCEDURE
Coastal Refinery Unit - Mechanical Maintenance
Revision 5 (sample document for demonstration, not a real standard)

1. SCOPE
This procedure covers routine and preventive maintenance of horizontal centrifugal process pumps.

2. ROUTINE CHECKS
2.1 Daily: check for abnormal noise, seal leakage, bearing housing temperature and lube oil level.
2.2 Weekly: record vibration at the drive end and non-drive end bearings.
2.3 Every 3 months: carry out a routine inspection covering coupling condition, foundation bolts and mechanical seal flush lines.

3. VIBRATION LIMITS
3.1 Up to 4.5 mm/s RMS: acceptable.
3.2 Above 4.5 and up to 7.1 mm/s RMS: alert. Investigate within 7 days.
3.3 Above 7.1 mm/s RMS: stop the pump and changeover to the standby.

4. BEARINGS AND LUBRICATION
4.1 Bearing housing temperature must not exceed 80 degrees Celsius.
4.2 Change lube oil every 6 months or 4000 running hours, whichever is earlier.
4.3 Use only the lubricant grade listed on the pump data sheet.

5. ALIGNMENT
5.1 Check shaft alignment after any maintenance that disturbs the pump or motor.
5.2 Allowable misalignment is 0.05 mm offset and 0.05 mm per 100 mm angular.

6. MECHANICAL SEAL
6.1 Visible leakage of more than 5 drops per minute requires seal replacement.
6.2 Replace seal faces and secondary seals as a set.

7. BEFORE STARTING WORK
7.1 Obtain a work permit and apply lockout-tagout as per SOP-SAF-002.
7.2 Isolate suction and discharge valves, drain and depressurise the casing.
