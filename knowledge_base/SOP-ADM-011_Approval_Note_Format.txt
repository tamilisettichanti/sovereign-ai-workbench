SOP-ADM-011  FORMAT FOR APPROVAL NOTES
Coastal Refinery Unit - Administration
Revision 2 (sample document for demonstration, not a real standard)

1. PURPOSE
All proposals that need sanction from a competent authority are put up as an approval note. This procedure fixes the format so that notes are consistent and can be decided quickly.

2. SECTIONS OF AN APPROVAL NOTE
Every approval note must contain the following sections, in this order:

Subject: one line stating what approval is sought.
Reference: the inspection report, work order or letter that the note is based on.
Background: two or three sentences on the equipment or matter and why the note is raised.
Key Findings: the facts, with measured values, listed as bullet points or a table.
Assessment: each finding compared with the applicable SOP limit, stating acceptable or not acceptable.
Proposal: the action recommended, with timeline.
Financial Implication: the estimated cost and the budget head.
Approval Sought: the specific approval requested and the competent authority.

3. DELEGATION OF POWERS FOR MAINTENANCE EXPENDITURE
Up to Rs 5 lakh: Deputy General Manager (Maintenance)
Above Rs 5 lakh and up to Rs 25 lakh: General Manager (Technical Services)
Above Rs 25 lakh: Executive Director (Refinery)

4. STYLE
4.1 Keep the note within two pages.
4.2 State measured values with units. Do not use vague words such as "some" or "a few".
4.3 Mark the note CONFIDENTIAL when it contains cost or vendor information.
